our story

A little shop with a seriously twisted imagination.

Totally Twisted started with one stubborn idea: dessert should be an adventure, not a ten-second decision at a counter. So we handed you the lever, chase the swirl, and let you pile on whatever makes you grin.

Tucked into Melville, we've grown from a self-serve froyo bar into a full-on twisted eatery — organic açaí bowls, choco kebab, gourmet bites, and desserts you won't find anywhere else on Long Island.
