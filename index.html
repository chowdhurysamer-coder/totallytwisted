our story

A wall of flavors. A bar full of everything.

We're a self-serve froyo shop on Schwab Road in Melville. Grab a cup, pull the lever on flavors like California Tart, Chips Ahoy, Banana and Peanut Butter, then hit the topping bar — cookie dough, fresh fruit, gummies, Nutella, Reese's, six sauce guns, the works.

There's more than froyo: self-serve açaí bowls, our Chocolate Strawberries board, and a freezer stacked with Totally Twisted and Tipsy Scoop pints. Grab a punch card at the counter — buy 10, get one free.
