our story

Fourteen flavors. One very long topping wall.

We're a self-serve froyo shop on the Route 110 corridor. You grab a cup, pull the lever on any of 14 rotating flavors, and hit the topping wall — cookie dough, boba, hot fudge, sour patch, the works. Want to taste before you pour? Ask for a tester. We hand them out all day.

Over the years we've added self-serve açaí bowls, choco kebab shaved to order, and a freezer stocked with Tipsy Scoop's boozy pints. Open late, cleaned obsessively, and the music stays at a normal volume.
